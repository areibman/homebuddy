# KIVIK — Gunnared beige

Compared against the exact [IKEA catalog photograph](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/images/products/kivik-sofa-gunnared-beige__1577008_pe1032893_s5.jpg?f=s) and [published product](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/kivik-sofa-gunnared-beige-s89499703/).

The previous GLB omitted both the fabric base color and its procedural weave, so the browser showed a white sofa. The rear support was too high, cushion faces were too flat, the backrest was too upright, and the elevated generic camera exaggerated seat depth.

Reconstructed the wide low arms, lowered the rear support behind the loose cushions, and rebuilt each seat/back cushion with curved faces, eased corners, slight textile tension and separate fine seams. Corrected the backward lean. Embedded a beige melange fabric color image and tangent normal image; the GLB no longer relies on Blender-only procedural shading. Published 2.28 × 0.95 × 0.83 m envelope is preserved.

The exact fabric scan, cushion wrinkles, hidden frame joinery and manufacturing tolerances cannot be recovered from this single catalog view. These details remain inferred, not certified manufacturer geometry. The original photo is kept unchanged in the viewer.

Browser fidelity pass: baked ambient occlusion to a second UV atlas, embedded in GLB, preserving contact depth at joints and seams in real-time lighting. Native PBR color maps remain unchanged.

Final browser calibration: textile/wood albedo corrected independently of the native studio lighting; occlusion texture filtered and set to 0.78 strength.
