## Form design process

### Winner of Red Dot Design Award 2016

The LISABO series has received one of the world’s finest design awards – Red Dot Design Award. The extremely practical wooden tables suit all rooms in the home and combine modern construction with timeless design.

## Quality

### Inspired by the skies. Built for your home.

How do we get all the beauty of wood without all the weight? Looking to the sky solved our design puzzle. Inspired by aircraft engineering, LISABO table features a lightweight, accordion-shaped core made from high-density fiberboard. It helps us use less material but offers great strength and stability. Giving you a table that’s easy to move around and strong enough to handle whatever you throw at it. Sometimes, the best design ideas come from looking in unexpected places.
